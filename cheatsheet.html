Skript Cheat Sheet

Commands


command /hello:
    trigger:
        send "Hello %player%!" to player

Events


on join:
    send "&bWelcome %player%!" to player
on death:
    send "%player% died from %death cause%" to all players

Variables & Loops


set {coins::%player%} to 10
add 5 to {coins::%player%}
loop all players:
    send "Hi %loop-player%" to loop-player

Conditions


if player has permission "admin":
    send "&aYou are admin!" to player
else:
    send "&cNo permission!" to player

Effects


send "Hello" to player
give player diamond sword
set player's health to 20
kill player
broadcast "%player% died!"

Tips


- Always indent 4 spaces under triggers/loops/conditions
- Use 'is' instead of '=' in if statements
- Place %placeholders% only in send messages
